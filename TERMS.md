## 1. Safety — read this part

**Trackside is a planning tool. It is not a safety system, and it does not
give you permission to be anywhere.**

Motorsport circuits are dangerous. Cars leave the track. Debris travels. Areas
that are safe during one session are not during another. Nothing in this app
changes any of that.

### 1.1 The app never authorises access

A location appearing in Trackside — including one marked "official" or
"public land" — is **not** permission to go there, from us or from anyone.
Access is granted by the circuit, the organiser, the landowner and the
marshals, and by nobody else.

### 1.2 Access information is user-entered and unverified

Every access classification in Trackside is typed in by a person: you, or
whoever shared a spot with you. It is **not** checked, approved or verified by
us or by any circuit. It may be wrong. It may have been correct once and since
changed — fences move, permissions lapse, and layouts are altered between
seasons.

Where nobody has recorded an access status, the app says so rather than
guessing. **Treat "unknown" as "find out before you go", never as "probably
fine".**

### 1.3 Instructions on the ground always win

If a marshal, a member of circuit staff, an organiser, a landowner or a
signpost tells you something different from what this app shows, **they are
right and the app is wrong.** Follow them. Leave when told to leave. Do not
argue with a marshal using your phone.

### 1.4 You decide where you stand

You are solely responsible for deciding where to position yourself, and for
your own safety and that of people around you. Use your own judgement about
run-off, barriers, escape routes, marshal posts and the direction cars travel.
Do not rely on this app for any of it.

### 1.5 Times, routes and walking estimates are approximations

Session times come from published timetables that change, often on the day.
Walking estimates are calculated over a simplified map and take no account of
crowds, closed gates, mud, queues, or the tunnel being shut. Sunrise and sunset
are astronomical calculations and say nothing about whether the light is
usable, or whether you can get where you are going in time.

**Leave more margin than the app suggests.** Being five minutes early costs you
five minutes. Being five minutes late costs you the session — and hurrying
across a circuit is how people get hurt.

## 2. What Trackside is

Trackside helps photographers at motorsport events record where they have shot
from, plan where to be and when, follow a timetable, and keep track of which
cars they have photographed. It works offline, because the places it is used
have no signal.

It stores everything on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).
